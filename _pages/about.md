**Assistant Professor** · Department of Educational Psychology · University of North Texas

Jihyun Lee is a quantitative methodologist whose research focuses on handling missing
data in meta-analysis, missing covariates in meta-regression, multiple imputation and
multilevel imputation methods, and structural equation modeling. Her work has appeared in
journals including *Psychological Methods*, *Research Synthesis Methods*, and
*Multivariate Behavioral Research*, and she currently serves as Associate Editor of
*Research Synthesis Methods*.
